This is a personal blog, and I want to be clear about what happens when you visit it.

## Analytics

I use **Google Analytics 4** to understand how many people read the blog, which posts they read, and where they came from (for example, LinkedIn). It collects:

- Pages viewed and time spent on them
- Approximate location (country and city), device and browser
- The site that referred you here

Google Analytics sets cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_*`) to tell new visitors apart from returning ones. It doesn't log or store your IP address. I can't see who you are, I don't run ads, and I don't sell or share this data.

Detailed analytics data is automatically deleted after 14 months.

If you'd rather not be counted, you can block cookies for this site, use a content blocker, or install [Google's opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Everything else

- **Fonts** are hosted on this site, so your browser doesn't contact Google Fonts or any other font service.
- **Hosting:** the site runs on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes, as described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- There are no comments, forms or accounts, so I don't collect anything you type.
