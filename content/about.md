I'm Vishal, a data engineer in the making, based in Bangalore.

I spent about five years at Capgemini supporting AXA Insurance, first as a Major Incident Manager and then in Problem Management. Most of that job was working out why systems broke and writing it up so it wouldn't happen again.

Alongside that, I moved into automation engineering. I taught myself Python and automated six operational reports that teams had been building by hand, cutting weekly report generation time by 75% and freeing up 14–16 people from manual reporting work. That's what pulled me towards data.

In 2024 I finished an MSc in Data Analytics at the National College of Ireland with First-Class Honours, and I'm now moving into data engineering.
